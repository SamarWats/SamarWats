## 💡 Projects & Research  

### 🤖 **AI Resume Builder and Smart Suggestions** | [Live Demo](/)  
The **AI Resume Builder with Smart Suggestions** helps users create professional resumes quickly. It offers **customizable templates**, **AI-powered content recommendations**, and real-time editing. With features like PDF export, smart keyword optimization, and easy management through a personal dashboard, it ensures resumes are impactful, polished, and tailored to job roles.
.  

#### 🔹 **Key Features:**  
✅ **Resume Creation & Editing** – Interactive form-based resume builder, Dynamic sections like Education, Experience, Projects, Skills, and Achievements.  
✅ **AI-Powered Smart Suggestions** – Keyword optimization based on target job role/industry.  
✅ **Multiple Resume Templates** – Professionally designed resume templates, Customizable colors, fonts, and layouts  
✅ **Dashboard for Resume Management** – View all created resumes in one place, Options to Preview, Edit, Delete, and Download resumes.  
✅ **ATS Compatibility** – Ensures resumes are Applicant Tracking System friendly.  

**Tech Stack:** React.js | MongoDB-Atlas | Tailwind | Puppetter | OAuth | JsonWebToken  



### **Bharat Compliance** | (CA client handling Dashboard) | **[Live Demo](/)**  
A **next-gen AI assistant** for developers to streamline project management and collaboration.  

✅ **User Dashboard** – Displays monthly compliance tasks and progress, filter tasks by month and completion status.  
✅ **Document Upload & Management** – Upload essential documents (Aadhar, PAN, GST Invoice, etc.), stored securely in the backend with database references.  
✅ **Task & Compliance Tracking** – Fetches tasks dynamically from the backend, tracks completion percentage.  
✅ **Admin (CA) Features** – Manage compliance tasks for users, approve/reject user services.  
✅ **My Services Section** – Users can view services they opted for, Payments allowed only after approval through **Razorpay**.  

**Tech Stack:** Next.js | TypeScript | MongoDB | JavaScript | Node.js | Express | JWT  



### 🍔 **Hotel Booking and Management System**  | [Live Demo](/)  
The **Hotel Booking and Management** System is a full-stack web application enabling users to browse, book, and review hotels and restaurants. It features secure JWT authentication with role-based access for users, sellers, and admins. Sellers manage listings, while admins oversee bookings and platform activity, ensuring a seamless booking experience.
 
✅ **Hotel & Restaurant Browsing** - Filter listings by location, price, amenities, and ratings.  
✅ **Booking & Reservation Management** - Manage reservations with options for modifications or cancellations.  
✅ **Review & Ratings System** - Users can submit reviews and rate hotels/restaurants.  
✅ **Admin Dashboard** - Approve/reject listings and ensure platform compliance.  
✅ **Cancellation & Refund** - Vendors get notified of cancellations and availability updates.  
**Tech:** ReactJS | CSS3 | Tailwind | JavaScript | JSON Web Tokens | MongoDB Atlas | Multe
